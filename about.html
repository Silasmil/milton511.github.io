    WHO I AM

    About

    Cybersecurity analyst, red team specialist, and Cisco IT Essentials instructor. I bridge the gap between technical security expertise and teaching clarity.

    What I do

    Find what attackers find

    Threat detection, incident response, vulnerability assessment, and penetration testing. I work across web, mobile, smart contracts, and infrastructure — whatever the target actually is, not just the part that's easy to test.

    Teach it so it sticks

    I build hands-on security labs and workshops that take learners from theory to practice. Cisco IT Essentials instructor. Design and content creation are part of the training, not separate from it.

    What I believe

    Write it down

    Every challenge I solve gets a writeup — including the dead ends. The "what failed and why" section is the part that teaches. Screenshots and flags are the easy bit.

    Real impact, not CV padding

    A single First Blood on a hard challenge is worth more than 50 "I'm passionate about cybersecurity" sentences. I aim for evidence over adjectives.

    Teach the fix

    Every writeup ends with defensive fixes because the vulnerability isn't the lesson — the fix is. The flag proves I solved it; the fix proves I understand it.

    Security is for everyone

    The best security work I've done was teaching beginners. Accessibility isn't a downgrade — it's the point. Making cybersecurity skills accessible is the actual mission.
