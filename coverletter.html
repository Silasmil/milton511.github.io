    Dear Hiring Manager,

    I am a cybersecurity analyst and Cisco IT Essentials trainer with a strong foundation in operating systems, ethical hacking, network administration, and system administration. My passion lies in securing systems, identifying vulnerabilities, and helping organizations strengthen their digital resilience.

    Over time, I have built hands-on experience in vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, and threat detection. I also enjoy mentoring and training others in IT and cybersecurity, simplifying complex technical concepts for learners and teams.

    Most recently, I have been publishing detailed CTF writeups from the Perfect Root (AfricaHackon) platform — nine writeups across web, mobile, smart contract, crypto, and forensics, including a First Blood on Echo Signature (ECDSA signature malleability). Each writeup goes beyond the exploit: the approach, the dead ends, and the defensive fix that would have prevented the vulnerability in the first place. That last part is what I think matters most.

    I am also a Cisco IT Essentials instructor, where I have designed and delivered hands-on lab components built around real equipment and troubleshooting scenarios. This experience has taught me that good security work is also good teaching work — the best finding is the one the team can actually act on.

    My goal is to continue developing my expertise in cybersecurity operations and contribute to creating safer digital environments. I am eager to take on new challenges that allow me to apply my technical skills, problem-solving mindset, and commitment to continuous learning.

    Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to your team.

    Sincerely,
